The short version

This website collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no comments, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. The only thing it stores is your light or dark theme choice, in your own browser.

This policy explains that in more detail. It applies to aaryavsharma-portfolio.vercel.app only. My project websites are separate sites with their own notes.

Who I am

This site is run by Aaryav Sharma, a high school student in Apple Valley, Minnesota, United States. It is a personal portfolio, not a business. You can reach me at shar0674@umn.edu.

What I collect

Through the website itself, nothing. It does not ask you to type in your name, email or any other detail, and it has no sign-up or login.

The contact page has a box that opens your own email app with your message filled in. That message goes from your email account to mine only if you press send in your email app. The website never sees it or stores it.

If you email me, I will receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you. I do not sell it, share it or add it to any list. I delete emails when I no longer need them.

What my hosting company can see

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel keeps ordinary server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the service secure and running. I do not use those logs to identify visitors. Vercel’s handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. It saves one small item in your browser’s local storage, called theme, so your light or dark choice is remembered. Details are on the cookie policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to places that are not run by me, including my project sites on vercel.app, GitHub, Google Drive (for my resume) and my textbook on GitHub Pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices and may set their own cookies, so please read their policies. I am not responsible for what they do.

My project websites run their calculations in your browser. For example, EEG Artifact Scope analyzes a recording on your own device and uploads nothing.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has emailed me personal information, tell me and I will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete from the site itself. If you have emailed me and want me to delete your message or tell you what I kept, write to shar0674@umn.edu and I will do it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you extra rights, and you can use them by emailing me.

Security

The site is delivered over HTTPS and uses security headers such as a strict content security policy. No system is perfectly secure, but because the site holds no personal data, there is very little to protect.

Changes and questions

If I change how the site works, I will update this page and the date. The current version is always the one on this page. Questions? Email shar0674@umn.edu.
