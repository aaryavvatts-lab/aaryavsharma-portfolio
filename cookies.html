The short version

This site does not use cookies. It saves one small preference in your browser so your light or dark theme is remembered. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking technology, so there is no cookie banner.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website can save in your browser. Local storage is a similar space that a site can use to save settings on your device. Both can be used to remember things between visits.

What this site stores

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
themeLocal storage, first partyRemembers whether you picked the light or dark theme.Until you clear your browser data

That is the complete list. The item holds the word “light” or “dark” and nothing else. It is not sent anywhere and it does not identify you. Because you ask for it by pressing the theme button, and it only makes the site work the way you chose, it does not need your consent under the usual cookie rules.

What this site does not use

  • No advertising or marketing cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No social media buttons or embedded videos that set their own cookies.
  • No fingerprinting.

Other sites you may visit

If you follow a link to another website, such as my project sites, GitHub or Google Drive, that site may set its own cookies. They are covered by that site’s policy, not this one.

How to control it

You can delete the saved theme any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. You can also block cookies and site storage in your browser. If you do, the site still works. It just will not remember your theme.
