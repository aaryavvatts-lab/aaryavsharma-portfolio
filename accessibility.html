Accessibility

How this site works for different people and devices, and how to tell me when it does not.

On this page

  • My goal
  • What I have done
  • Known limits
  • Tell me about a problem

Last updated October 20, 2026

My goal

I want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control or magnification. I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What I have done

  • A “Skip to content” link and clear page landmarks.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Text meets at least a 4.5 to 1 contrast ratio in both the light and dark themes.
  • The site follows your device’s reduced motion setting. With it on, animations are turned off.
  • Text can be enlarged and the layout still works down to a 320 pixel wide screen.
  • Pictures have descriptions for screen readers, and links that open a new tab say so.
  • The light and dark themes follow your device setting, and you can switch them yourself.

Known limits

The project demos live on separate sites, and some of them draw charts or images that are harder to describe in words. Each one tries to give a written summary next to the visual, but I may have missed some. If something does not work for you, I want to know.

Tell me about a problem

If you have trouble using any part of this site, email shar0674@umn.edu with the page and what happened. I will reply within a few days and fix what I can, or find another way to give you the information.
