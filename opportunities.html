What I’m looking for

I am a sophomore, so I know most of this is a couple of years early. I would rather say what I want than wait until it is normal to ask.

Research positions

A neuroscience, neural engineering or brain-computer interface lab where I can help with data analysis, reading papers or building pipelines. In person in the Twin Cities is easiest, but I can do computer work remotely, and I already have a working setup for EEG, ECoG, fMRI and MRI data.

Neurotech internships

An internship or summer role at a company that works on brain-computer interfaces, EMG or EEG wearables, or neural monitoring devices. I am building my own idea in this area, and I want to learn from a team that has already shipped something.

Mentorship

A grad student, postdoc or founder who will look at my work and tell me what I am getting wrong. I would rather hear it now than after I have built more on top of it.

Collaboration and competitions

Co-writing a paper, entering a science fair or research competition together, or teaming up on a hackathon. I have the tools and the habit of checking my own work, and I want to point that at a harder problem.

If any of this sounds like you, email me.
