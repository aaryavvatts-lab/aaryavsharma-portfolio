Research & medical exposure

July to September 2026

Lab assistant

Moriarty/Weber Lab, University of Minnesota Twin Cities

I worked under Dr. Richa Kaushal in a gene editing and cell therapy lab that sits under the Crane Lab. My day-to-day covered iPSC culture, turning iPSCs into macrophages, media prep, gene editing, passaging cells and running maxipreps.

Before I started, I made my own study guide on iPSCs, iPSC-derived macrophages, flow cytometry and cell surface markers, and I kept a running log of my shifts.

September 2025 to present

Neurosurgery mentorship

Dr. Anant Naik, University of Minnesota

I have watched two live neurosurgeries with Dr. Naik and seen each full procedure from inside the operating room. Through this mentorship I started looking into caregiver burden and communication gaps in glioblastoma (GBM) treatment, which became the NeuroBridge GBM project below.

May 2026 to present

NeuroBridge GBM

HOSA Medical Innovation project

I am building a plan for caregiver communication and symptom tracking in glioblastoma care. So far I have cold-emailed more than 150 neuroscience and medical professionals for interviews and clinical feedback, and I have run surveys and calls to learn what caregivers really struggle with.

September to December 2025

ThinkNeuro research cohort

I studied computational neuroscience, including EEG classification and machine learning, and read hundreds of papers on EEG activity and brain function. I presented a research poster to a panel of judges.

Leadership

December 2025 to present

NeuroGIDAS

Founder and co-president, Eastview High School

I started NeuroGIDAS to make neuroscience easier for any student to get into. It is our school’s chapter of GIDAS (Genes in Diseases and Symptoms), a miRcore program where students use real genetic data to study how diseases work. More than 15 students come to our meetings, and I lead a team of 5.

I wrote the proposal that got us a faculty advisor, and I came up with a statewide Brain Cancer Innovation pitch competition that the club is now working to run.

Founder and co-president

South Asian Student Association

Eastview High School

Growing up, I felt scared to show my culture, and sometimes I did not want to. I started SASA so there would be a place at Eastview where everyone could share theirs openly and feel proud of it. More than 20 students are in the club, and I lead a team of 4.

We bring students together around food, music, festivals and stories, and we keep it open to everyone who wants to learn.

July 2026 to present

Flow State Framework

Co-lead, Outreach Committee

Flow State Framework is a Minnesota debate tutoring organization that offers free coaching and tutoring to middle and high school students. I co-lead its 4-person Outreach Committee. We have grown active membership by 18 students and built partnerships with 3 local schools and community organizations.
