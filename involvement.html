Research

Lab and clinical exposure

Lab assistant, Moriarty/Webber Lab

University of Minnesota Twin Cities

I work under Dr. Richa Kaushal in a gene editing and cell therapy lab, sitting under the Crane Lab at UMN. My day to day covers iPSC culture, differentiating iPSCs into macrophages, media prep, gene editing, passaging, and running maxipreps. I built my own study guide on iPSCs, iPSC derived macrophages, flow cytometry, and cell surface markers before starting, and I keep a running log of my shifts.

Neurosurgery observation

Dr. Anant Naik

I have observed two live neurosurgeries under Dr. Anant Naik.

Clubs and initiatives

What I've helped build

Neuroscience Club

Eastview High School, co-founder

I helped launch the club and worked on its structure from the start. I put together a faculty advisor proposal, and I developed the concept for a statewide Brain Cancer Innovation pitch competition the club is working toward.

Northstar Math Collective

Founder and Executive Director

A UMTYMP tutoring and mentoring initiative I am incorporating as a Minnesota nonprofit. I lead a founding team of three other high schoolers who each run a different piece: curriculum and instruction, partnerships and outreach, and programs and operations. We are working from a week by week launch plan running from August through December 2026.

NeuroBridge Minnesota

Youth-led health policy initiative

A policy institute I am helping develop, built around two projects: the Minnesota Neurological Access Index, a county level database of neurological care access, and the CareCoord Protocol, a documentation framework for caregivers.

FlowState Frameworks

Outreach Co-Lead

A Minnesota student-run debate mentorship organization based in Eagan, offering free one on one coaching in Congressional, Lincoln-Douglas, and Policy debate. I lead growth focused outreach, including cold email and school outreach campaigns, with a standing weekly target of five new students or volunteers.

HOSA and Science Olympiad

Active member

I compete in both. I also play tennis.
