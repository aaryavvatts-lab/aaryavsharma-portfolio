Involvement

Research, and two organizations I've built

The projects page covers work I do alone. This page covers where I show up for other people, and where other people are counting on me.

Research

Lab and clinical exposure

Lab assistant, Moriarty/Webber Lab

University of Minnesota Twin Cities

I work under Dr. Richa Kaushal in a gene editing and cell therapy lab, sitting under the Crane Lab at UMN. My day to day covers iPSC culture, differentiating iPSCs into macrophages, media prep, gene editing, passaging, and running maxipreps. I built my own study guide on iPSCs, iPSC derived macrophages, flow cytometry, and cell surface markers before starting, and I keep a running log of my shifts.

Neurosurgery observation

Dr. Anant Naik

I have observed two live neurosurgeries under Dr. Anant Naik, watching each full procedure from inside the operating room. It is a different side of neuroscience than the one I get from public datasets on my laptop: seeing what the surgery actually asks of a surgeon, in real time, with no room to redo a step.

Organizations I've built

BioSphere Learning and NeuroGIDAS

BioSphere Learning

Founder

An education hub I founded that connects students with tutoring organizations, so students looking for a tutor and tutoring programs looking for students both have one place to go. It has grown to more than 1,000 followers on Instagram.

NeuroGIDAS

Eastview High School, co-founder

I helped launch NeuroGIDAS, the neuroscience club at my school, and worked on its structure from the start so it would be more than a group that just meets to talk about the brain. I wrote the proposal that got it a faculty advisor, and I developed the concept behind a statewide Brain Cancer Innovation pitch competition, which the club is now working to actually run. The goal is to give students who are curious about neuroscience a real project to build, not just a lecture to sit through.
